# Macha

Macha is named for the figure of that name in the Ulster Cycle. In *Noínden Ulad*, a pregnant Macha is forced to race the king of Ulster's horses, wins, gives birth, and curses the men of Ulster with debilitating pangs. The curse is the traditional explanation for their incapacity during *Táin Bó Cúailnge*, leaving Cú Chulainn to defend Ulster.

Irish tradition contains more than one figure called Macha; the name here is a reference, not an attempt to collapse them into one character.

I liked the name, the Ulster connection, and the fact that most people will never notice any of this.

The spelling is intentional. It is not Mocha.
